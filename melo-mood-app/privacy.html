隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 27 日 · 適用於 Melo Mood 網頁版（以下稱「本服務」）

先講重點：你的日記、心情分數和錄音檔，只存在你自己的裝置上，不會傳到我們的伺服器。只有你主動登入、訂閱，或使用需要雲端的聲音與 AI 功能時，才會有少量資料離開你的手機，下面逐項說明。

1. 只存在你裝置上的資料

  • 名字、目標、日記、感恩清單、心情分數、練習紀錄、前後評分、收藏、設定、你錄的聲音檔。
  • 存放在瀏覽器的 localStorage 與 IndexedDB。我們沒有這些資料的副本；清除瀏覽器資料、換手機或刪掉主畫面圖示，資料就會消失，所以請定期用「我 → 匯出備份」。

2. 登入與訂閱時，我們會處理的資料

資料為什麼保存多久
Email用登入碼登入；方案跟著帳號走直到你刪除帳號
方案、狀態、到期日決定你能用哪些功能直到你刪除帳號
每月用量（真人聲音字數、AI 次數）公平使用額度直到你刪除帳號
付款紀錄（訂單編號、金額、時間、綠界回傳的交易資訊）對帳、退款、稅務與法令要求依法令保存，不含你的 Email，帳號刪除後仍會保留

信用卡號由綠界科技（ECPay）處理，不經過我們，我們看不到也不會儲存。

3. 使用雲端功能時，資料會送到哪裡

功能送出什麼送給誰

相機與麥克風：只在你按下「錄音」或「坐上 Melo 雲自拍」時，經你的同意才開啟。自拍的去背在你的手機上完成，照片不會上傳。

4. 我們不做的事

  • 不放廣告、不做行為追蹤、不使用第三方分析工具、不出售或出租你的資料。
  • 不會把你的日記用來訓練任何 AI 模型。

5. 你的權利

  • 刪除帳號：「我 → 方案 → 刪除帳號」。會停止之後的扣款，並刪除你的登入帳號、方案紀錄、用量、AI 聲音（含 ElevenLabs 上的聲音與用它合成的音檔）。
  • 刪除 AI 聲音：「我 → 方案 → AI 複製你的聲音 → 刪除」。
  • 備份與帶走資料：「我 → 匯出備份」。
  • 想查詢、更正或有其他要求，請寄信到下方聯絡信箱。

6. 兒童

本服務不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意蒐集他們的資料。

7. 這不是醫療服務

Melo Mood 是陪你練習的工具，不是醫療、心理治療或診斷。如果你現在很辛苦：安心專線 1925（24 小時）、生命線 1995、張老師 1980。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 Oct 2026 · Applies to the Melo Mood web app ("the Service").

In short: your journal, mood scores and recordings stay on your own device and are not sent to our servers. Only when you sign in, subscribe, or use the cloud voice and AI features does a small amount of data leave your phone.

On your device only

Name, goals, journal, gratitude lists, mood scores, practice history, ratings, favourites, settings and your voice recordings live in your browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). We hold no copy. Use "Me → Export backup" before switching phones.

What we process when you sign in or subscribe

  • Email (login by one-time code), plan, status and renewal date, and monthly usage counters (voice characters, AI calls) — kept until you delete your account.
  • Payment records (order number, amount, time, transaction data returned by ECPay) — kept as required for accounting and law; they do not contain your email and remain after account deletion. Card numbers are handled by ECPay and never reach us.

Cloud features and third parties

  • Glow voices: the sentence to be read is sent to ElevenLabs through our server; the audio is cached in our private storage so identical sentences are synthesized once.
  • Muse voice clone: your 30–60 second sample (only after you tick consent) is sent to ElevenLabs through our server. We do not keep the sample, only the voice ID.
  • Muse AI scripts and weekly review: short excerpts of your recent journal entries and practice stats are sent to Anthropic through our server for that single request. We do not store them.
  • Your own ElevenLabs / Claude keys (optional): stored only on your device; requests go from your device straight to those services.
  • Google Fonts, Spotify embeds and jsDelivr (selfie cut-out model) see your IP address when those parts load.

Camera and microphone are used only when you tap record or the cloud selfie, with your permission. Selfie cut-out runs on your phone; photos are not uploaded.

What we do not do

No ads, no behavioural tracking, no third-party analytics, no selling of data, and no training of AI models on your journal.

Your rights

Delete account: Me → Plan → Delete account (stops future charges; removes your account, plan record, usage, and your AI voice including the copy at ElevenLabs). Delete AI voice: Me → Plan → AI voice → Delete. Take your data: Me → Export backup. Other requests: write to the contact address below.

Children

The Service is not designed for children under 13.

Not medical care

Melo Mood is a practice tool, not medical care, therapy or diagnosis. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (Taiwan: 1925, 1995, 1980).
